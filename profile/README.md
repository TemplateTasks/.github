# 👋 Welcome to TemplateTasks

Hello there, and thanks for stopping by!

I'm [Vadim Starichkov](https://github.com/starichkov/), a software engineer passionate about building useful, reusable and ready-to-use solutions for everyday development needs.

This is my personal initiative — [TemplateTasks](https://www.templatetasks.com) — where I publish microservices, libraries, and demos that solve common problems developers face over and over again across projects, teams, and companies.

## 💡 The Idea

We often end up reimplementing the same patterns: Kafka consumers, mail integrations, service templates, etc.  
TemplateTasks exists to **implement once, share openly, and help others reuse efficiently**.

Thus, the moto:

> Implement. Share. Reuse.

And a quote that sums it up:

> Insanity is doing the same thing over and over and expecting different results.
